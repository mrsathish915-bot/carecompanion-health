EVERYDAY NUTRITION

Food & Wellness Guide

General ideas to discuss with your healthcare professional and adapt to your needs.

🥦
BLOOD SUGAR CARE

Eating with Diabetes

  • Build balanced meals with vegetables, protein, and suitable portions of carbohydrates.
  • Choose high-fibre foods such as beans and whole grains when appropriate.
  • Limit sugary drinks and follow your individual meal plan.
🥑
BLOOD PRESSURE CARE

Eating with High BP

  • Choose fresh foods and reduce excess salt as advised.
  • Include vegetables, fruits, and other balanced foods suited to your needs.
  • Check with your clinician before using salt substitutes, especially if you have kidney problems or take certain medicines.
🍲
HEALTHY AGEING

Everyday Nourishment

  • Choose foods that are easy and safe for you to chew and swallow.
  • Include protein-rich foods as recommended for your needs.
  • Follow any personal dietary, allergy, or fluid restrictions.
ℹ️

Remember: These are general educational suggestions, not a personalized diet or treatment plan. Your clinician or dietitian can advise you based on your conditions, medicines, and nutritional needs.
